KHRECOM Seed Generator

Builds a seed mod for Kingdom Hearts Re:Chain of Memories. Install the downloaded zip in OpenKH as its own mod, next to KHRECOM-LUA-LIBRARY and KHRECOM-RANDOMIZER, and start a new game. The same seed and the same settings always build the same mod.

Seed

Starting deck

13 cards, at least one attack card, always under Sora's 275 starting CP. The healing cards are Cure, Bambi, Tinker Bell and the enemy cards Oogie Boogie, Vexen, Neoshadow, Search Ghost and White Mushroom.

Floors

Every floor then offers one fixed world. The floor groups are 2 to 6 and 7 to 10. Bosses only have proper stats on the floors they normally appear on.

Enemies and bosses

Every boss brings its own reward. Riku's bosses are Lexaeus, Zexion and Ansem. This is experimental: big bosses were made for their own arena and may look wrong, get stuck or crash. Keep the spoiler log to see which boss was where.

Randomize enemy stats: the largest change in percent, different for every enemy. 0 is off.

Rewards

Everything set to Yes adds its cards to one card pool and its sleights to one sleight pool, and both pools are then shuffled over the places they came from. Enemy drops add their cards to the same pool.

Boss rewards are the cards of every boss, Guard Armor and the other big ones too, and the sleights that come with your first copy of a magic or summon card, so your starting cards can give shuffled sleights. Story rewards are the cards and sleights from events and 100 Acre Wood. Friend sleights are given without a popup by the story scene where the friend joins, so whatever lands there is silent too. A friend sleight found somewhere else still needs that friend's card in battle.

This covers chests, objects, bounties, Rooms of Rewards, enemy drops, Moogle shops, boss and story rewards and the starting deck, randomized or not. Enemy cards have no value. Friend cards in battle are not changed. If you already hold 99 copies of a card, the game can still give a nearby value.

Doors ask for map card values, so without high values or 0 some doors may never open.

Power

Card power covers attack, magic, summon and friend cards. Attack cards have a strike, a thrust and a finisher power. Power is a percent of Sora's attack for most cards and sleights. A few values are something else: Gravity takes a percent of HP, Stop and stunning moves hold a time, and some are the number of hits.

Story doors and map cards

Key to Rewards can still drop, and while you hold 9 Random Jokers the game gives other map cards. Random Jokers only exist at value 0, so the enemy map card value settings are not used.
